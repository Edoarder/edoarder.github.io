About me

I am Edoardo Maggioni, a computational social scientist interested in how collective patterns emerge from interactions between people and the systems they inhabit.

View my CV ↗

Background

I am completing the National PhD in AI for Society at the University of Pisa, with doctoral research at IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca and IIT-CNR. My thesis is approved and my defence is scheduled for 13 November 2026.

My training in theoretical physics led me towards complex systems and the study of social interaction. At ETH Zürich, I developed an agent-based model to investigate structural balance and geometrical frustration in signed social networks.

During my PhD, I worked on the organisation of online debate, climate communication and pro-environmental behaviour, and multilayer communication networks in energy governance. A research visit to the IT University of Copenhagen brought together social-media analysis, Eurobarometer surveys, official statistics, and NLP.

How I work

I enjoy moving between methodological and substantive questions: developing an analysis, understanding what a pattern means, and connecting it to a wider social context. My work combines network science and statistical modelling with the practical challenges of integrating heterogeneous data.

I am drawn to collaborative, interdisciplinary research, especially where ideas from statistical physics can help make social systems more understandable. Communication, collective behaviour, sustainability, and the use of evidence in policy are recurring threads.

Research community

I am part of the organising team of Fostering Research Synergies, an informal network of PhD students and postdoctoral researchers. We organise workshops and conferences that give early-career researchers space to share ideas, meet across disciplines, and build collaborations.
