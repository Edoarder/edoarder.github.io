Networks & higher-order pathways

Beyond Direct Retweets: Multi-Step Pathways in Italian COVID-19 Twitter

Longer pathways redistribute attention unevenly across communities, revealing structural roles that direct retweets and community size alone do not capture.

Edoardo Maggioni, Ren Manfredi, Fabio Saracco, Rossana Mastrandrea

Submitted manuscript · Social Networks · 2026

arXiv ↗ PDF ↗ Code ↗
About the study & my contribution

How does the picture of an online debate change when we look beyond direct links? Using approximately 3 million retweets involving 334,633 accounts, we combine statistically validated community reconstruction with random-walk motifs on a weighted, directed retweet network. The analysis compares direct connections with multi-step endpoints, revealing community persistence, directional asymmetries, and shifts in prominence across the debate.

My contribution. I developed and implemented analyses of community-level motif profiles, persistence, and cross-community pathways, and contributed substantially to writing the manuscript.

Network reconstruction · Random-walk motifs · Community-level analysis

The paths characterise connectivity in the aggregated network; they do not reconstruct individual tweet cascades.

Digital traces & collective behaviour

Twitter climate discourse as a signal of pro-environmental behaviors

Climate-related tweet density is associated with regional differences in pro-environmental behaviour, linking online attention with independent survey evidence.

Edoardo Maggioni, Diego Garlaschelli, Rossana Mastrandrea, Luca Maria Aiello

Revision in progress · EPJ Data Science · 2026

arXiv ↗ PDF ↗ Research Square ↗ Code ↗
About the study & my contribution

Can online climate discourse help us understand regional differences in environmental behaviour? This study brings together geolocated Twitter data, Eurobarometer responses, and Eurostat indicators across European regions. Statistical models and NLP-based measures connect the volume and content of online discourse with self-reported environmental actions.

My contribution. I built the data integration and regional analysis workflow, applied NLP classifiers, and carried out statistical modelling, robustness checks, visualisation, and manuscript writing.

Statistical modelling · Geospatial data integration · NLP

Digital communication infrastructures in Irish offshore wind governance: A multilayer network analysis

Distinct communication layers reveal differences in connectivity alongside a shared structural backbone, bringing visibility, brokerage, and fragmentation into focus.

Edoardo Maggioni, Nuccio Ludovico, Marc Esteve del Valle, Franco Ruzzenenti, Rossana Mastrandrea

Under review · Energy Research & Social Science · 2026

Code ↗
About the study & my contribution

How do different communication channels connect the organisations involved in an energy transition? We analyse institutional hyperlink and Twitter networks around Irish offshore wind, comparing stakeholder groups, communication roles, and the structures shared across layers.

My contribution. I developed the network analysis, cross-layer comparisons, and visualisations, and wrote the methods and results in collaboration with the project team.

Multilayer network analysis · Network projections · Cross-layer comparison
