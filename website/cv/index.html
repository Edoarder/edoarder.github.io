Thesis

Networks in Society: Complex Systems Approaches to Digital Communication and Environmental Change

Supervisors: Rossana Mastrandrea and Diego Garlaschelli

The thesis examines how digital communication networks shape collective behaviour and socio-environmental change. Across case studies on COVID-19 information pathways, European climate discourse and pro-environmental behaviour, and Irish offshore-wind governance, it combines higher-order pathways and multilayer network analysis with survey data and official statistics to connect online communication structures with broader behavioural and institutional dynamics.
