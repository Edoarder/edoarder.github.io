Research interests

I bring a statistical-physics perspective to social questions: how networks organise attention, how online discourse relates to behaviour, and how local interaction rules shape larger systems.

I am interested in extending this work to multilayer and longitudinal networks, social simulation, and interpretable evidence for policy.
